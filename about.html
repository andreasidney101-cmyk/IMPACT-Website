Intégration Multisensorielle Perception Action CogniTion

The IMPACT team (Intégration Multisensorielle, Perception, Action et CogniTion) is one of the 21 teams of the Lyon Neuroscience Research Center (CRNL). Its research aims to understand the behavioural processes and neural mechanisms involved in multisensory perception, motor planning and execution, cognitive representations and executive control: functions that are essential to human behaviour.
