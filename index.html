A little introduction

About me

I am a PhD researcher in neuroscience at the Lyon Neuroscience Research Center (CRNL), within the IMPACT team. My doctoral research is conducted in collaboration with the University Hospital of Montpellier and focuses on the sense of agency, depression, and suicidal behavior.

Areas of interest

Research interests

Sense of agency

How we perceive ourselves as the authors of our actions.

Clinical neuroscience

Understanding psychological and neural processes in depression and suicidality.

Experimental methods

Behavioral experiments, electroencephalography (EEG), and computational analysis.
