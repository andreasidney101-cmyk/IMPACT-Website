General information

Intégration Multisensorielle Perception Action CogniTion

The IMPACT team (Intégration Multisensorielle, Perception, Action et CogniTion) is one of the 21 teams of the Lyon Neuroscience Research Center (CRNL). Its research aims to understand the behavioural processes and neural mechanisms involved in multisensory perception, motor planning and execution, cognitive representations and executive control: functions that are essential to human behaviour.

Respect &
wellbeing at the lab

Welcome to the IMPACT team! Here are a few recommendations to help us all build a pleasant, respectful and safe working environment, for the members of our team as well as for the visitors and volunteers we welcome at the lab.

1

Let’s respect every person at the lab

🧍Let’s respect everyone’s physical integrity.
🔒Let’s respect privacy and private life.
🤝Let’s respect the dignity of all.
💬Let’s adopt respectful communication and behaviour.

You will find recommendations on the CRNL wiki, under:

Services et Groupes Transversaux→Égalité→Nos documents→Quelques clés pour de bonnes relations au travail
↗

Discrimination, harassment and any behaviour that undermines people’s dignity are not tolerated.

2

Let’s welcome volunteers in good conditions

☎️To contact and recruit volunteers, let’s use a work phone and a work email address as much as possible, or even an address dedicated to recruitment.
🧑‍💼When an experiment involves welcoming a volunteer, let’s make sure a colleague is informed, reachable and nearby if needed.

These practices help guarantee an appropriate working and welcoming environment, in the interest of everyone involved.

3

If you face a difficulty, don’t stay alone!

If you are facing an uncomfortable situation, inappropriate behaviour or a relationship difficulty, you can talk to someone you trust. Several people are available to listen, advise and guide you, in a confidential setting.

Within IMPACT

  • Andréa Auvray, PhD student, for students.
  • Your PI or another PI.
  • Fadila Hadj-Bouziane and Alessandro Farnè, directors of IMPACT.
Within the CRNL

With your supervising institutions (CNRS, INSERM, Université Lyon 1, Université Lyon 2, etc.)

Who does what?
Research organisation
CNRS
Centre national de la recherche scientifique

A national public organisation that carries out and supports research in many fields. It employs researchers, engineers and technicians, who often work in labs shared with universities.

Research organisation
INSERM
Institut national de la santé et de la recherche médicale

A national public organisation dedicated to research on human health, including how the brain works, neurological and psychiatric disorders, and the biological mechanisms of health.

Universities
UCBL & UJM
Université Claude Bernard Lyon 1 · Université Jean Monnet

Public universities that provide higher education and take part in research. They host students, PhD students, lecturer-researchers and research staff, notably in health and neuroscience.

Joint lab (UMR)
CRNL
Centre de Recherche en Neurosciences de Lyon

A joint research unit (UMR) under the shared supervision of these four institutions, which pool their staff, resources and expertise. That is why your colleagues may be employed by the CNRS, INSERM or a university, while all working in the same lab.

Practical information about renting

Agency fees

If you rent through an estate agency, some fees may be charged. They are regulated and capped for the services concerned, so they are not necessarily equal to one month's rent.

Security deposit

Unfurnished: at most one month's rent excluding charges.
Furnished: at most two months' rent excluding charges.

Notice period when you leave

Furnished: one month.
Unfurnished: usually three months, reduced to one month in “high-demand areas” (zones tendues) such as Lyon, provided you follow the required formalities.

Income requirements

Landlords and agencies often ask for a monthly income of about three times the rent. This is common practice, not a general legal requirement. A guarantor or a rental guarantee scheme can help your application.

Rent control

Rent control applies in Lyon and Villeurbanne. Check that the rent you are asked for respects the legal caps for that home.

your IT access

Request your institutional account

When you join the IMPACT team, contact the administrative office to start the process of creating your institutional account (INSERM, CNRS or university, depending on your situation).

This account gives you access to your institution's digital services, including your work email and administrative tools.

Get your CRNL login

You will also need CRNL IT credentials, separate from your institutional ones. They give access to some internal lab resources, including the storage server and the CRNL Wiki.

Wi-Fi: eduroam

To connect to the eduroam Wi-Fi network, use your email address and your INSERM password (or those of your own institution if you are not employed by INSERM).

If it doesn't connect
  1. Go to cat.eduroam.org ↗
  2. Download the installer, then search for Inserm (or your own institution).
  3. Open your device settings: the downloaded eduroam profile appears there. Install it.
  4. Enter your credentials (email address and password).

Mattermost, the lab's messaging app

Mattermost is a collaborative messaging platform, similar to Microsoft Teams or Slack, that makes communication and teamwork easier. You can exchange messages, share documents and organise discussions in different topic channels (projects, announcements, events, etc.).

In a research lab, Mattermost is a way to talk with your colleagues, keep up with team news and coordinate projects.
